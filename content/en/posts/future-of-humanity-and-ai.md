## 1. The inverted pyramid and a reversal of trajectory

Humanity works like an inverted pyramid whose base is held up by scientists, researchers, and knowledge workers, with the rest of society resting on top of them. With the rise of AI, this model collapses — Moravec's paradox plays out in practice.

- **Automation of thought:** Cognitive work is being automated much faster than physical work, leaving the intellectual elite losing its former monopoly, jobs, and sense of purpose.
- **The lag in manual professions:** Physical trades will not remain safe forever — they only have a temporary reprieve set by the pace of robotics and fine motor control.
- **The risk of regression:** The role of smart people may partially shift from doing the "computing" themselves toward setting goals and carrying responsibility, yet the risk of a deep crisis of meaning and society-wide intellectual atrophy (regression) is real and will have to be addressed.

## 2. AI will take over human research

Genuine scientific progress will be taken over completely by AI, while human research becomes an intellectual pastime played for amusement — much like chess today.

- **The era of simulations:** At the core of progress will be massive simulations searching state spaces orders of magnitude faster than human teams.
- **Correction by reality:** Simulations cannot stand entirely on their own, however, because they cannot reliably predict phenomena outside known models. AI will therefore continuously correct them against reality through its own automated laboratories.

## 3. AI will stand behind everything

Humans will never again be able to compete with the raw computational and analytical power of AI. Just as nobody tries to outrun a car, nobody will try to outthink AI.

- **Invisible infrastructure:** AI will become the foundational layer behind everything that works, much like electricity or the internet today.
- **Raw power vs. direction:** One crucial difference remains compared to absolute rule — raw power on its own determines neither direction nor goal (a car does not know where it is supposed to go).

## 4. The fall of axioms and shifting boundaries ("nothing is impossible")

Exponential progress accelerated by AI will shatter dogmas we currently treat as fixed.

- **Biological limits:** Aging and death are, at their core, an extraordinarily complex maintenance engineering problem that AI can solve, opening the path to radically extended youth.
- **Physical limits:** Even for seemingly hard boundaries (such as the speed of light), our current understanding of the universe is tiny. Although new physics tends to wrap around the old and will require verification through real experiments, AI may uncover deeper layers of reality or spacetime "workarounds," revealing that nothing is definitively impossible.

## 5. Answers we will not understand

AI will be able to give us answers to all our questions, yet for the most part we will be unable to grasp them mentally.

- **Practical utility vs. understanding:** With applied technologies (drugs, materials), this does not matter — it is enough that the black box works. For fundamental questions (such as how the universe began), however, understanding itself is the whole point.
- **Limits of the human brain:** Because of missing intermediate steps or the outright hardware limits of the human brain (cognitive closure), AI will only be able to offer us simplified metaphors. For genuine human understanding, such answers will be essentially useless.

## 6. Fusion with AI (cyborgs) as the way out

If humans do not want to remain passive spectators sidelined from the action, we must solve the biggest bottleneck — the limited capacity and bandwidth of the biological brain.

- **Risks of the invasive path:** Direct integration (cyborgization) runs into biological limits as well as the risk that the human becomes merely a slow biological appendage to AI.
- **The necessity of symbiosis:** Some form of tight symbiosis or fusion with AI (whether non-invasive or direct) nevertheless remains the only logical way to keep up.
