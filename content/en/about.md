This site is a personal notebook on artificial intelligence — written in both English and Czech by [Ondra Linek](https://ondralinek.cz/).

## What you will find here

- **Field notes from building with AI** — what holds up in real codebases and production systems, and what breaks once the demo ends.
- **Agents and tooling** — how autonomous coding workflows, evals, and context engineering change day-to-day software work.
- **Longer essays** — reflections on craft, judgement, and where human intent still matters most when machines write the code.
